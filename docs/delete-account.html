Request the permanent deletion of your HR Insight account and associated personal data.

Overview & Policy

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data processed by the HR Insight mobile application.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, your user profile, mobile tokens, and app authentication access will be removed.

🗑️ Data Deleted Upon Request

  • Account credentials & profile photos
  • Mobile device registration & push tokens
  • Personal contact information & phone numbers
  • Saved app settings & preferences

📁 Data Retained (Employer Policy)

  • Historical company payroll & tax audit records
  • Approved attendance logs legally required by labor laws

Submit Deletion Request

Fill out the form below to automatically open your email app addressed to fourthpyramid21@gmail.com, or copy the request details.

⏱ Processing Time: Deletion requests are processed within 7 to 30 business days after verifying user identity with your employer's HR administrator. Confirmation will be sent to your registered email.
