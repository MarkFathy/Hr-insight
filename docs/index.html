Privacy Policy

Official Document

1. Introduction

Welcome to HR Insight, a mobile human resource management application developed and operated by Fourth Pyramid ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and protect your personal information when you use the HR Insight application.

By registering, logging in, or using HR Insight, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate HR, attendance, and organizational operations, HR Insight collects the following categories of data:

  • Personal Identity Information: Full name, employee identification number, job title, department, email address, and contact phone number provided by your organization or created during registration.
  • Location Data (Precise & Approximate): GPS coordinates and network location when checking in or out of work shifts, verifying presence at designated company geofenced offices, or logging location-based attendance.
  • Camera & Photo Gallery Access: Images captured or uploaded through your mobile device when setting up a profile picture or uploading supporting documentation for leave requests, expense claims, or HR reports.
  • Technical & Device Data: Mobile device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and app usage logs for diagnostic and security audit purposes.

📍 Important Notice regarding Location Data: HR Insight accesses your location ONLY when performing attendance clock-in/out or map location picker actions inside designated workplace features. Location data is used strictly for employment attendance verification and is never sold or shared with external advertising networks.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process collected data exclusively for valid business and HR purposes, including:

  • Verifying employee identity and authenticating secure user sessions.
  • Processing office arrival/departure times, shift logs, and attendance reports.
  • Facilitating leave applications, manager approval workflows, and employee directories.
  • Ensuring system security, preventing unauthorized access, and debugging application errors.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We respect your privacy and enforce strict confidentiality boundaries:

  • Your Employing Organization: Your profile, attendance logs, and submitted requests are accessible to authorized HR administrators and managers within your designated employer's organization.
  • No Commercial Data Selling: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks under any circumstances.
  • Service Infrastructure: Data is securely transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted channels to our backend cloud servers.

5. Data Security

Fourth Pyramid employs industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards—including encryption in transit (SSL/TLS) and secure token storage—to protect your personal data against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, loss, or access.

Mobile App Permissions

App Disclosures

HR Insight requests specific permissions on your Android or iOS device to enable core features:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used to confirm that you are within designated office bounds when clocking in or out.
  • CAMERA: Used to take real-time photos for employee profile pictures or document scanning.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Used to let you choose existing photos or files from your gallery to attach to HR requests.
  • INTERNET: Required for secure communication with company HR API services.

Account & Data Deletion Request

Google & Apple Compliant

In compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store developer policies, HR Insight provides users with full control over their account data, including the right to request permanent account deletion and data removal.

⚠️ Warning: Requesting account deletion will permanently remove your login credentials, personal profile details, and mobile access tokens. Note that mandatory employment historical records (such as past approved tax or payroll audit logs) may be retained by your employer as mandated by local labor laws.
